01 / WHO I AM

More than just the numbers.

Hi, I'm Roel Valenzuela — a Data Analytics Professional and WFM Senior Team Leader with over a decade of experience in operations, workforce management, and analytics.

My journey is driven by curiosity and a passion for understanding how things work. I enjoy transforming complex data into meaningful insights that help businesses understand challenges, uncover opportunities, and make more confident decisions.

Today, I continue expanding my knowledge in Machine Learning and Generative AI, exploring how these technologies can help create smarter solutions and solve increasingly complex business problems.
