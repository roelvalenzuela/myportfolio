01 / WHO I AM

More than just the numbers.

Data helps me understand the world. Curiosity makes me explore it.

RV

Hi, I'm Roel Valenzuela, a Data and Business Analytics Professional, currently working as a Senior Team Leader for Global Command Center Real-Time Operations at a leading global payments technology company.

With over a decade of experience, I've built my career across data and business analytics, operations, workforce management, and real-time operations—while leading teams, improving processes, collaborating with stakeholders, and using data to drive better business decisions.

I've always been curious about how things work and why they work the way they do. That curiosity naturally led me toward data, problem-solving, and finding the stories hidden behind numbers.

Working at the intersection of people, operations, data, and technology has taught me that the best solutions don't always come from simply looking at a spreadsheet. They come from understanding the bigger picture, asking better questions, and connecting different perspectives.

But great ideas and insights only matter when they can be turned into action. I enjoy collaborating across teams and with stakeholders, bringing people together around a problem, and turning insights into practical solutions that deliver meaningful results.

Whether it's improving a process, solving an operational challenge, or using data to support better decisions, I believe the best outcomes come from a combination of curiosity, collaboration, and execution.

I don't just look at what the numbers are saying. I look for why they're saying it—and work with people to turn those insights into meaningful action.

But curiosity doesn't clock out.

Outside of work, my curiosity takes me in completely different directions. Music, movement, fashion, languages, technology — I genuinely enjoy starting with something unfamiliar, being a beginner, and slowly figuring it out.
